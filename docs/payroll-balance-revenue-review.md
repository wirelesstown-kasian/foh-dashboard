# Payroll adjustment balances and revenue review — November 26

Paid payroll corrections now compare revised entitlement with the persisted `paid_amount`, rather than with the last edited entitlement. Positive means additional pay is due; negative means an employee overpayment is available for refund or carry-forward. Saving a correction alone does not move cash. A manager explicitly records a payment/refund or keeps the balance open. Cash movements and employee settlement events commit with the correction in one transaction. Check/ACH settlements are recorded by the manager without inventing bank transactions.

The next Wage Worksheet lists prior outstanding employee credits. “Apply credit to this payroll” includes the amount in deductions and links the old employee credit to the new payroll item on save. Credits are never applied automatically. The database locks settlement operations, verifies employee/period identity, rejects amounts above the remaining credit or outside the new deductions, and rolls back failures. An application can be removed before saving. Existing Check Writer duplicate-number protections and issued payroll snapshots remain unchanged.

Migration `20261126000000_payroll_balances.sql` baselines existing records from their currently saved payout amounts. It does not infer earlier payments/refunds from free-text notes. If an earlier correction overwrote its original payout, that original history needs supporting payment records before it can be reconstructed. It does not change employee pay rates, roles, schedules, clock records, or existing payroll entitlement amounts.

November read-only audit at implementation time: 20 EOD records through 2026-11-24; gross $193,502.94 = net $153,724.11 + sales tax $14,734.12 + tips $25,044.71. No November imported sales periods were present. The gross formula already sums saved EOD gross once; no unsupported sales correction was applied. The dashboard's misleading “cash + credit + tax + tips” caption was replaced with “Net revenue + sales tax + tips,” source count/latest EOD are visible, and the gross card now displays cents rather than rounding to whole dollars. A different POS expected total/date range still requires reconciliation against those sources; missing EOD days are not forecast into actual gross.

Checks omit asterisks before the numeric amount. Every printed memo now includes the transaction category, or `Payroll - (start to end)` for payroll, followed by an optional additional memo. Standard autogenerated payroll-date text is omitted when redundant. The memo is shared by check face, stubs, register and CSV.

Verification covers temporary Postgres migration execution, persistent balances after reopening, partial refunds and additional payments, carry-forward deductions, invalid/duplicate-credit rollback, cash movement counts, check PDF output, November period boundaries, tax/tip presentation, clock/DST handling, tips, daily guarantees, wage reconciliation, historical/dashboard reports, and payroll calculation dependencies. Production verification uses a rolled-back transaction, preserving all real payroll values.
